SECTION 01
ORIGIN & EARLY LIFE

Subject was born in Nevada, later relocating to Pillkill, NV — a township that does not appear on any state-issued map and is listed in no census. Its existence is confirmed only by streaming service metadata and a single gas station receipt recovered from a murder scene.

At an undetermined point in his adolescence, subject became fixated on actor Matthew Lillard, consuming his filmography on a compulsive loop. This obsession escalated following repeated viewings of the 1996 film Scream. Subject legally attempted to change his name to MATTHEW THE FRICK LILLARD — request was denied by the Clark County clerk on grounds of "disturbing intent."

The obsession did not stop at cinema. Subject began to identify with the film's primary antagonist, eventually moving from identification to imitation.

SECTION 02
KNOWN CRIMINAL HISTORY

Subject's first confirmed victim was his own mother. Details of the incident are too graphic for standard filing. Following this event, subject systematically eliminated the remainder of his bloodline — his grandmother, his sister — before expanding his activity beyond family units.

Subject has been shot a confirmed multiple times across various incidents. He has survived every instance. PKPD ballistics team has no explanation for this. Dr. ████ ████████ of the Nevada State Medical Board described his continued survival as "physiologically impossible and deeply upsetting."

The name "Buffalo Pill" was assigned to subject by a victim during an active incident who reportedly said, "What are you? Some sort of buffalo pill?" Subject paused, replied "Huh. Buffalo Pill. I think I like that name," and completed the encounter. PKPD considers this the official origin of the alias.

Most notable incident: Las Vegas, NV — committed in collaboration with associate known only as "K-DOT" (see associated file PKPD-204-KD). Exact nature of the Las Vegas incident remains classified above this clearance level. What we can confirm: it was bad.

SECTION 04
PSYCHOLOGICAL PROFILE

Subject presents as perpetually angry with documented episodes of emotional vulnerability, particularly regarding loss and romantic failure. Despite his actions, subject displays capacity for genuine grief. He is afraid of two things: losing, and being made fun of.

Subject has been married twice. Both marriages ended under circumstances PKPD has declined to document in this file. He owns a dog named Scooby Doo, whom he loves unconditionally. Agents who have surveilled subject report that his demeanor in the presence of the dog is "completely different. Like a different guy entirely. It's weird."

Subject also maintains a deep personal attachment to his knife. Forensics have declined to run further tests on it.

Profiling note: subject does not appear to feel remorse for his crimes in the conventional sense. However, his fourth studio album suggests he is beginning to process the consequences of having eliminated his entire family. PKPD regards this as a potential vulnerability. It may also just make him angrier. We genuinely do not know.

SECTION 06
PILLKILL, NV — LOCATION REPORT

Pillkill, Nevada does not exist according to any official state or federal record. It does not appear on Google Maps, Apple Maps, or any cartographic database maintained by the USGS. It has never been incorporated as a municipality, township, or census-designated place.

And yet: it is listed as subject's hometown on three separate streaming platforms. Two PKPD officers dispatched to investigate its coordinates in 20██ did not return. Their vehicles were found approximately 40 miles outside of ████████, NV, engines still running, doors open.

The name "Pillkill" appears in zero of subject's songs. He has never referenced it directly. PKPD analysts believe this is intentional. "He wants you to find it yourself," wrote one analyst before resigning from the department the following morning.

Current PKPD position: Pillkill is real. We do not recommend visiting. We do not recommend thinking about it too hard. You are already thinking about it too hard. Stop.
